# Design reference and generated assets

The user selected the brushstroke-cloud homepage mockup, preserved as `approved-homepage.png`. It establishes typography/layout, sky hero, photographic windows, striped resources, paint clouds, burgundy pep talk, and clean post cards. The homepage is rebuilt as HTML/CSS, not displayed as a screenshot.

The following assets were generated with the built-in image generation tool. WebP copies in `public/images` are optimized for the website without creative alteration. Original generated files remain in the generator's library. All image generation is complete; no image-generation API is called by the site.

## sky.webp

Prompt: Create a photorealistic wide landscape background photograph of a soft washed blue daytime sky with fluffy white cumulus clouds along lower edges and a few wisps at top. Pale clear blue center and left for black website text overlay, airy calm natural sunlight. Pure sky only. No text, no illustrations, no objects. Website hero background inspired by a casual editorial fashion portfolio.

## desk.webp

Prompt: Photorealistic editorial lifestyle photograph, landscape 4:3: warm sunlit cream desk with a ceramic coffee mug near the center, silver laptop entering from the right, a closed ivory notebook at left, beautiful long window shadows on warm plaster wall. Quiet study atmosphere, subtle film grain, natural muted colors, no people, no text, no logos. Premium casual magazine photography.

## book.webp

Prompt: Photorealistic editorial photograph, landscape 4:3: closeup of an open book on a warm ivory desk beside the edge of a silver laptop at upper right, slim dark pen resting near book, dramatic soft late morning window shadows, quiet aspirational study mood. Realistic paper and shallow depth of field. No people, no added text or logos. Premium casual magazine photography.

## cloud.webp

Prompt: Create ONE isolated wide flat cloud silhouette built from broad overlapping opaque gouache brush strokes, pastel sky blue pigment, subtle natural bristle ridges and irregular painted perimeter. Wide cloud with 3 to 4 rounded top lobes and relatively flat bottom, generous smooth light-blue center to place a black label later. Sophisticated editorial handmade paint mark, NOT cartoon character, no face, no outline, no text, no objects, no shadow. Cloud fills most canvas with small clear margin. Genuinely transparent background.
